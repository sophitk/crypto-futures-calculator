How it works

  • Notional value = entry price × position size.
  • Initial margin = notional value ÷ leverage.
  • Profit/loss and ROI are gross, before fees. Estimated trading fees use entry and exit notionals.
  • Liquidation uses a simplified isolated linear model with no extra margin or maintenance deduction. It excludes funding, slippage, exchange risk tiers and close fees. Verify with your exchange.
